# Rank Up Neo, the light-grid world: Codex prompts

14 images (sections 1 to 7). Paste the STYLE block in front of every prompt. Save each one into `assets/rank-up-neo/art/` under its file name, overwriting what's there. Everything stays original: no film, game or brand.

The sky and the skyline come out colourless (neutral grey/white light) on purpose. The app tints them cyan for Silver and gold for Gold, and runs a colour wave across them as the world changes. That way both worlds are one picture and line up exactly.

---

## STYLE (in front of every prompt)

```
Original sci-fi "digital frontier" world: a dark virtual world where light is the only colour. Matte black
and deep graphite surfaces, glossy obsidian panels, and thin, perfectly clean strips of emissive light inlaid
in the geometry, each with a white-hot core fading out to its colour. Cinematic, minimal and premium, as
clean as an Apple keynote: huge contrast, deep true blacks, no clutter, no grunge, no noise, no lens dirt,
no bloom haze. Original design, not based on any existing film, game, character or brand. No text, no
letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark.
```

## 6. `titles.png`: the league titles

Both titles go in one image so they come out in the same lettering. The app cuts it into single letters (they drop in one at a time) and tints them: SILVER LEAGUE cyan, GOLD LEAGUE gold.

```
2048x1024, genuine transparent background. Two lines of display lettering, centred, one above the other
with plenty of space between: "SILVER LEAGUE" on the top line and "GOLD LEAGUE" on the bottom line. Exactly
those letters, spelled exactly, all capitals.

Original lettering (not any existing film logo or typeface): wide, extended, geometric capitals built from
hollow tubes of light. Each letter is an outline drawn as a double line of light, a bright outer tube and
a thinner inner one, with squared corners turned on small tight radii. A few deliberate small breaks are
notched into the strokes, and the horizontal strokes run slightly long. The light is pure WHITE: a white-hot
core with a soft glow that fades out to fully transparent close round each stroke. Every letter has the
same cap height on one shared, perfectly level baseline, with even, generous spacing: a clear gap of empty
transparent space between every two letters, nothing touching or overlapping, so each letter can be cut out
on its own. No reflection, no floor, no other glow, no other words.
```

## 7. `buttons.png`: the buttons

One image, so the three come out as one family. The app cuts them apart and stretches the two wide ones to any width (9-slice), so their long middle stretch has to stay plain. All the detail sits in the end caps.

```
2048x1536, genuine transparent background, three button frames laid out with lots of empty space between
them. They are empty frames: NO text, NO icons, NO arrows, nothing in the middle.

1) Top: a wide button 1600x320. A slab of dark smoked glass (#0B1014, slightly see-through) with its four
   corners cut off at 45 degrees (chamfered), edged all the way round with a crisp double line of CYAN
   light (#33E6FF, white-hot core): a bright outer line and a fainter inner one. Near each end, a short
   brighter bar of light along the bottom edge and a small notch in the top edge. The long middle stretch
   between the ends is perfectly plain and the same all along, edges straight, so it can be stretched.
   A faint glossy reflection along the top inside edge.
2) Middle: exactly the same button, same size and shape, but its light is warm GOLD (#FFC24A, white-hot
   core).
3) Bottom left: a small square button 320x320, the same chamfered dark glass, edged only with a single
   thin hairline of soft neutral grey-white light, no glow: the quiet one.

Light glows only along the edges, fading out within a few pixels; no haze outside the frames. Straight-on,
flat front view, no perspective, no shadow, no floor.
