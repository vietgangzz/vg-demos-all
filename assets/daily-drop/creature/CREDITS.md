# Ember Drake's body

`dragon.json` is "Dragon Evolved" by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), downloaded from Poly Pizza
(https://poly.pizza/m/LlwD0QNUPj) as a GLB and stored base64 encoded so Metro bundles it as JSON.

License: CC0 1.0 (public domain).

The app recolours it and sets its tail and breath on fire (src/demos/daily-drop/creature.ts).
